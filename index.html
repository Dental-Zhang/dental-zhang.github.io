Abstract

I am a pediatric dentist at Suzhou Huaxia Stomatological Hospital, working on the prevention of dental caries in children. During my M.Med. at Sun Yat-sen University under Prof. Huancai Lin, I showed that poly(γ-glutamic acid) can act as a non-collagenous protein analog, stabilizing amorphous calcium phosphate and inducing hierarchical intrafibrillar mineralization of type I collagen. I have also contributed to studies of the oral microbiome in severe early childhood caries. Since 2024 I have been an implementer of the Suzhou Pediatric Oral Health Epidemiological Survey.

1.2PGA-mediated biomimetic mineralization of type I collagen

Master's project · Sun Yat-sen University · 2020.09 – 2023.06

  • Synthesized poly(γ-glutamic acid)-stabilized amorphous calcium phosphate (PGA-ACP) with varying molecular weights and concentrations; fabricated 2D thin-layer matrices and 3D type I collagen scaffolds from rat tail tendon.
  • Tracked dispersion stability by optical density, pH, particle size and zeta potential, validating PGA as a non-collagenous protein analog that induces hierarchical intrafibrillar mineralization.
  • Characterized mineralized scaffolds with TEM, SEM, XRD, FT-IR and TGA.
